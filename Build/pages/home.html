Welcome

A beautiful place to call home.

Blue Valley Acres is more than a point on the map. It is a community of people who value the land around them, the neighbors beside them and the chance to live a little closer to Colorado's open country. We are glad you are here.

02 · Location

Between the river and the high country.

Blue Valley Acres sits along Colorado Highway 9 between Kremmling and Silverthorne. The surrounding country offers a strong sense of space and a close connection to the landscape. Explore the map, learn about nearby communities and find the directions you need before you travel.
