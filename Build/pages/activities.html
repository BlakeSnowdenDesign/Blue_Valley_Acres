Activities

Ten ways to spend a day in the Blue Valley.

Hunting, fishing, skiing, hiking, rafting and golf are all within an easy drive of Blue Valley Acres.

01

Hunting

Some of the best hunting experiences in Northern Colorado. From big game like elk and deer, to waterfowl and turkeys, to mountain lion and black bear, we have it all. Whether you’re a beginner or an experienced hunter, you’ll find the perfect adventure here.

02

Fishing

Blue Valley Acres is the perfect destination for fishing aficionados looking for the best fly-fishing and fishing spots in the state. We have some of the best lake and high mountain fishing, as well as fly-fishing in some of the best rivers in the area. Join us for the best fishing experience of your life.

03

Skiing

Blue Valley Acres is the perfect destination for skiers looking for the best skiing experience in Colorado. We are located within an hour drive of all the major ski areas in northern Colorado, including Breckenridge, Copper, Vail, Beaver Creek, Winter Park, Steamboat, Mary Jane, A-Basin, and more.

04

Hiking

If you’re looking for some of the best hiking in the United States, we have it nearby. Blue Valley Acres sits in the heart of northern Colorado near the Continental Divide, surrounded by Rocky Mountain National Park, the Eagles Nest Wilderness and hundreds of thousands of acres of National Forest and BLM land. Whether you’re a seasoned hiker or a beginner, you’re sure to find something to satisfy your wanderlust.

05

Rafting

Right in Kremmling there are 5 major rafting companies offering unforgettable rafting experiences. The Colorado River will take you through the majestic headwaters starting at the western side of Rocky Mountain National Park. The trips feature beautiful canyon scenery, whitewater rapids, spectacular wildflowers, sheer granite canyon walls, and forests of blue spruce and Douglas fir trees. Raft companies specialize in river adventure, rafting, kayaking and stand-up paddleboarding.

06

Golf

There are 11 world class golf courses close to Blue Valley Acres. The Raven Golf Club at Three Peaks is considered one of the top courses in the country and, without hesitation, the best mountain course in Colorado. Beaver Creek Golf Club was voted one of the Top 10 in the country which is a testament to its designer, Robert Trent Jones. The club at Cordillera is home to four very distinct golf courses, all designed by some of the greatest names in the world of golf – Jack Nicklaus, Tom Fazio and Colorado native Hale Irwin. Breckenridge Golf Club has three championship courses, the Bear, the Beaver and the Elk. All three nines are Jack Nicklaus Signature courses. Playing at 9300+ feet in elevation, each course offers a mountain golf experience. Panoramic views of the snowcapped Colorado Rockies can be seen on every hole.
