Frequently asked questions

Good questions to start with.

Plain answers, with links to the organization that can confirm details.

What is the Land Share?

Blue Valley Acres Land Share Inc. is the community organization for Land Share members. It looks after Land Share documents, community notices and common-area matters, and collects annual landowner dues.

How do I pay annual landowner dues?

Use the secure Pay Dues page. Enter your parcel number and owner information so the payment is credited to the correct property.

Can I fish or use the river?

River access and fishing depend on your property and the community's governing documents. Read River Access and Fishing before you go, and contact the Land Share if you are unsure.

Where do I find maps?

Maps and common-area information are in Member Resources.
