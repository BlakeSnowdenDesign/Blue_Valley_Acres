Fishing, river and common property use

The rules posted at the campground, in full. Respect the land at all times.

Common property
  • Pack out whatever you pack in.
  • Prevent pollution. Keep garbage, litter and foreign substances out of the river.
  • Do not carve, chop, cut, or damage any live trees.
  • Leave natural areas the way you find them.
  • Keep noise at a reasonable level.
  • Use existing fire rings. Always use proper campfire management and fire safety.
  • Use of fireworks or other explosives is prohibited within campground.
  • No discharge of firearms from Memorial Day through Labor Day.
  • Please dispose of human waste properly using “leave no trace” techniques.
On the river
  • Guests must be accompanied by a member.
  • Membership badge must be worn and visible from a distance at all times while on the river.
  • Only two (2) guests maximum per membership per visit to be fishing on the river at any time.
  • Introduce yourself on the river, particularly if you don’t see a membership badge being worn. If the person cannot properly prove their identity themselves as a member, or guest ask them to leave the property immediately.
  • Allow no poaching or illegal fishing; be proactive.
  • Floaters who are not members are not allowed by Colorado law to anchor, pull off, or get out of their boats, this is trespassing.

If needed contact Grand County Sheriff 970-725-3343 and the Colorado Division of Wildlife 970-725-6200.

Fishing rules and etiquette

BVA is a gold medal fishery and it needs to be maintained with care.

  • Fishing is catch and release with barbless single hooks only.
  • Do not “cut away” tippet, leader, used flies or lures, lead shot, strike indicators etc. into the river or on the banks.
  • Respect other members on the water, use fishing etiquette. Give space to anglers and always handle fish with care.
Proper fish handling

Many anglers release fish thinking that they will survive when, in fact, the fish will die a few days later due to issues created during the fight and release. Please utilize these simple steps to keep our section of the river prime.

  • Do not over play the trout, land the fish as quickly as possible to reduce acidosis.
  • When possible use either a rubber or coated nylon mesh net.
  • Allow the trout to rest in the water while you remove the hook.
  • Always wet your hands before you handle a fish.
  • Never pick up a fish by its gill plate.
  • Never keep a fish out of the water for more than 10 seconds.
  • Never lay a fish on the bank.
  • When releasing, work the fish slowly back and forth in the water, until you feel the fish try to kick free of your grasp. Keep working the fish until you are confident it has recovered.
  • A fish swimming off on the surface or on its side is not a good sign. If the fish is bleeding heavily from the mouth or from any portion of its gills the fish will die.
